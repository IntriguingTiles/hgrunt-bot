PinBot Privacy Policy

Here's how PinBot uses your data outside of Discord:

  • If a message is successfully forwarded to the pin channel, the message ID is saved to a database to ensure that a message isn't forwarded multiple times.

This data is retained for as long as the bot remains in the server.

When PinBot is removed from a server, all associated data and settings are permanently deleted.

Server staff may configure PinBot to forward pinned messages, either through Discord's pin feature or through reactions (if enabled), to a specific channel. This may include the following user data:

  • Your username and avatar at the time the message was forwarded.
  • The contents of the message, including any attachments and/or embeds.

As these forwarded messages go to a channel that the server staff control, it is up to the staff what they do with these messages, such as how long they last for, or if forwarded messages pertaining to you can be deleted. PinBot has no control over this.

No additional user data is collected or stored.
